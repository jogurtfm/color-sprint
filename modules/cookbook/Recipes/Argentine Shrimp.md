# Garlic-Butter Argentine Red Shrimp

Recipe ID: argentine-shrimp
Status: Ready
Servings: Not provided

## Ingredients

- 1.1 lb jumbo Argentine red shrimp, peeled, deveined, tail-off
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1/2 lemon (juice)
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (optional)

## Steps

### Thaw and dry

Thaw shrimp completely if frozen, drain, and pat dry with paper towels.

### Season

Season shrimp with paprika, salt, black pepper, and optional red pepper flakes.

### Heat skillet

Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp in a single
layer, working in batches if needed.

Temperature: Medium-high stovetop heat

### Cook first side

Cook for approximately 2–3 minutes on the first side. The timer prompts the first
check at 2 minutes; assess the shrimp before flipping.

Temperature: Medium-high stovetop heat
Timer: 2 min

### Flip and cook second side

Flip and cook another 1–3 minutes, until the shrimp are opaque and cooked through.
Avoid overcooking. The timer prompts the first check at 1 minute; extend if needed.

Temperature: Medium-high stovetop heat
Timer: 1 min

### Garlic butter

Reduce heat to medium-low. Add butter and minced garlic. Stir for 30–60 seconds
until garlic is fragrant, coating the shrimp in the butter. Check at 30 seconds.

Temperature: Medium-low stovetop heat
Timer: 30 sec

### Finish and serve

Turn off the heat. Add lemon juice and optional parsley; toss and serve.
