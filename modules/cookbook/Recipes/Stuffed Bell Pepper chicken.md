# Cheesy Chicken-Stuffed Bell Peppers

Recipe ID: stuffed-bell-pepper-chicken
Status: Ready
Servings: Not provided

## Ingredients

- 1–2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, diced into small cubes
- 3 large bell peppers, halved lengthwise and seeded
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup salsa
- Splash of water, as needed
- 1 cup shredded cheese, such as pepper jack or Mexican blend
- Cooked rice or beans (optional)

### Optional Toppings

- Sour cream or lime crema
- Diced avocado or guacamole
- Pico de gallo
- Pickled jalapeños
- Crumbled cotija cheese
- Chopped cilantro
- Crispy onions, tortilla strips, or crushed tortilla chips

### Optional Lime Crema

- 2 tbsp sour cream
- Squeeze of lime juice
- Pinch of garlic powder
- Dash of hot sauce

## Steps

### Preheat oven

Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C).

Temperature: Oven 400°F (205°C)

### Prepare and pre-bake peppers

Slice bell peppers lengthwise and remove seeds and membranes. Lightly coat with
olive oil, place cut side up in a baking dish, and pre-bake for 10–15 minutes to
soften. The timer prompts the first check at 10 minutes.

Temperature: Oven 400°F (205°C)
Timer: 10 min

### Season chicken while peppers bake

While peppers bake, dice chicken into small, evenly sized cubes. Toss with
paprika, garlic powder, chili powder, cumin, salt, and black pepper.

### Brown chicken

Heat the remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken;
brown for 2–3 minutes. The timer prompts the first check at 2 minutes.

Temperature: Medium-high stovetop heat
Timer: 2 min

### Stir and finish cooking chicken

Stir and cook another 3–5 minutes, until it reaches 165°F (74°C) internally.
Check at 3 minutes and verify the internal temperature with a food thermometer;
extend cooking if needed.

Temperature: Chicken internal temperature 165°F (74°C)
Timer: 3 min

### Add aromatics and salsa

Add diced onion and minced garlic. Cook 2–3 minutes, stirring, until softened and
fragrant. Stir in salsa and a splash of water to keep the filling juicy. Check
the aromatics at 2 minutes.

Timer: 2 min

### Fill pepper halves

If using cooked rice or beans, stir them into the chicken mixture. Spoon filling
evenly into the pre-baked pepper halves.

### Add cheese

Top generously with shredded cheese or pepper jack.

### Bake filled peppers

Bake for another 10–15 minutes at 400°F, until the peppers are tender and cheese
is melted and bubbly. The timer prompts the first check at 10 minutes.

Temperature: Oven 400°F (205°C)
Timer: 10 min

### Optional lime crema

If making lime crema, stir together sour cream, lime juice, garlic powder, and
hot sauce.

### Top and serve

Finish with any desired toppings and serve.
